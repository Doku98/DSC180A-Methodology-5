## Capstone Reflections

**1. What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**

One of the most interesting topics we explored was how the City of San Diego handles Get It Done reports. I was surprised by how complicated the routing process can be, especially when reports are sent between departments. I became interested in how data science could help identify these issues and reduce unnecessary delays.

**2. Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.**

I would like to investigate whether a multimodal language model could use both report descriptions and uploaded photos to identify incorrectly categorized reports. For example, a resident might select the wrong issue type when submitting a report. A model could potentially flag these cases for staff review before they are routed to the wrong department.

**3. What is a potential change you'd make to the approach taken in your current Quarter 1 Project?**

One change I would make is to focus more on evaluating the accuracy of individual reports rather than relying primarily on referral data to identify routing problems. Our current dataset does not contain the complete history of every transfer between departments, which makes it difficult to determine exactly when a report was misrouted. Using report descriptions and images could provide another way to investigate these issues.

**4. What other techniques would you be interested in using in your project?**

I would be interested in comparing traditional machine learning classifiers with multimodal large language models. For example, we could evaluate whether adding image information improves classification accuracy compared to using text alone. I would also like to explore confidence thresholds so that uncertain predictions are flagged for human review rather than automatically changing a report's category.
